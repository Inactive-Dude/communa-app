About Us

Welcome to Communa, the central hub for all college club activities.



We are Group 1, a collaborative team of student developers brought together by a shared passion for the principles of Object-Oriented Programming. Our primary mission for this project was to design and construct a robust application from the ground up, translating OOP theory into tangible, real-world practice.

Guided by the core pillars of OOP, we collectively engineered our system to be modular, scalable, and maintainable. We focused on leveraging Encapsulation to ensure data integrity, Inheritance to promote code reuse, and Polymorphism to build flexible and dynamic functionalities. Every aspect of this project, from the initial architecture to the final implementation, was a product of our shared effort and dedication.

This project was brought to life by the combined contributions of Aaron A George, Aditya Manojkumar, and Anantha Krishnan A R. We are proud to present a final product that reflects our deep appreciation for elegant and effective software design.
